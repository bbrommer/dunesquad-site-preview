What it does

What is DuneSquad?

DuneSquad is a golf trip planner app for buddies trips. One organizer sets up the itinerary, tee times, rides and dinners, and the whole squad sees the same plan on iPhone or Android. It also splits shared expenses in dollars, pounds or euros and keeps a photo feed for the trip, seen only by the trip and its visitors. There are no ads and no marketing or third-party analytics.

Games and the Standings board

Side games for the whole trip: ten formats, from the Team Cup (match play: foursomes, four-ball and singles) to Stableford, Skins, Nassau, Sixes, Wolf and Baseball. Every game feeds one Standings board.

  • One person keys in the scores for the group.
  • The Team Cup tracks each player's wins, losses and halves all trip.
  • Side games settled between friends, outside the app. DuneSquad never handles money.

The plan, where everyone can see it

Add the tee times once. Everyone sees the itinerary, and anyone can add it to their calendar.

  • Tee times, ride pickups and dinner reservations, day by day.
  • Each ride shows the pickup time, the driver and where to meet.
  • Each course has its scorecard and the weather for your tee time.
  • Subscribe in Google, Apple or Outlook.

Who owes who, squared up

Splitting expenses on a golf trip is easy: log shared costs as you go. Square Up shows each player's net and the fewest payments to settle.

  • Each expense in dollars, pounds or euros. Nothing is converted, and balances show per currency.
  • Tap to open Venmo, PayPal or Cash App with the amount filled in.
  • DuneSquad doesn't handle payments. It opens your payment app.

Photos and the trip feed

Photos and short videos from the course, with comments and reactions, seen only by the trip and its visitors, unless you choose to share a photo.

  • Family at home can follow along as visitors: the plan, the games and the photos, never the expenses.

FAQ

Golf trip planner FAQ

Does everyone see tee times, pickups and dinner without texting me?

Yes. Add them once and they're on the trip for everyone. Home shows what's up next, and anyone can subscribe in Google, Apple or Outlook. Each person can also choose which notifications they get, like a morning brief, an evening recap and a heads-up when their ride is leaving.

Does it work for team match play or our usual side games?

Yes. There are ten formats, including the Team Cup, Stableford, Stroke Play, Skins, Nassau, Sixes, Wolf and Baseball. One person keys in the scores, and every game feeds one Standings board.

Can it find the course info and scorecard for us?

Usually. When you open a course, the app looks for the scorecard and saves it marked "not yet confirmed" for you to check. You can also snap the printed card.

Can friends and family follow along?

Yes. Each trip has a visitor link. Visitors see the plan, the games and the photos, and can comment and react, but never see the expenses.

What does it cost?

One $69 Trip Pass covers the whole squad. Create your trip free; the pass unlocks invites. Split across eight people, including the organizer, that’s about $8.63 each, and less per person with a larger squad. Add the pass to your trip expenses to track everyone’s share and settle up outside the app.

Does DuneSquad handle the money?

No. DuneSquad doesn't handle payments. It works out who owes who and opens your payment app.
